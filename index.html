Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Kuper is an Android dashboard for browsing and using wallpapers and Kustom customization assets. Kuper does not ask you to create an account. The app's settings, favorites, and other app data are kept on your device. To show online wallpapers, the app connects to the configured content hosts, which will receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address.

Information and access

Kuper does not operate an account system or a developer-owned server for user profiles. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this build, and Droid Beauty does not receive app usage data through the app.

  • App data stored on your device: preferences such as theme and language, favorites, and cached content are stored locally so the app can work. They are not uploaded to Droid Beauty.
  • Online content requests: when Kuper retrieves the wallpaper catalog or an image, the relevant hosting provider receives the request, including your IP address and standard connection information. The catalog configured in this build is hosted on GitHub; image links may point to GitHub or other hosts selected by the catalog. Those providers may keep server logs under their own privacy policies.
  • Current wallpaper preview: if you choose to preview the device wallpaper behind an asset, Kuper reads the wallpaper available through Android on your device for that display. Kuper does not upload it.
  • Saving a wallpaper: when you choose Download, the selected image is fetched from its host and saved to your device's shared pictures storage. When you choose Set as wallpaper, Android applies the chosen image. These actions do not send your personal files to Droid Beauty.
  • Permissions and device features: the app uses network access to load content and Android's wallpaper and media APIs for the actions you request. It does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, or call-log access.

The exact content hosts can change when the app's wallpaper catalog or its image links change.

How information is used

Local app data is used to remember your preferences and favorites, display content, and support downloads or wallpaper application. Network requests are used to retrieve the catalog and images you view or select. Droid Beauty does not use this information to build advertising profiles or make automated decisions about you.

Sharing and third parties

Droid Beauty does not sell or share user data because the app does not send user account or usage data to us. Network requests go directly from your device to the relevant content host. GitHub and other image hosts process those requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

Kuper includes an optional integration for the Muzei live wallpaper app. If you connect Kuper to Muzei, Muzei can request the selected artwork from Kuper on your device. Muzei's own privacy practices apply to its app and services.

Android and Google Play services may process information as part of installing, updating, securing, or running Android apps. Those services are governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Kuper does not maintain a user account or a copy of your local app data on a Droid Beauty server. Android keeps Kuper's settings, favorites, and private cache on your device. You can clear the app's data or uninstall Kuper in Android settings to remove that app data. Cached files may also be cleared by Android or through the app's cache controls.

Images you explicitly download are saved outside Kuper's private app storage and can remain after uninstalling. Remove those images using your device's gallery or file manager. Applying an image as your system wallpaper is managed by Android; change it in your device's wallpaper settings.

Network providers may retain request logs according to their own policies and retention schedules. Droid Beauty cannot access or delete those provider logs; contact the relevant provider for requests about them.

Your choices

  • Use Android app settings to clear Kuper's local data or uninstall the app.
  • Choose whether to browse online wallpapers, download an image, enable Muzei integration, or preview the device wallpaper.
  • Use Android permissions and network settings to manage the app's access. Some features that need online content will not work offline.

Children

Kuper is a general customization utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It has no account registration and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Droid Beauty, contact us so we can review the request.

Security

Kuper uses Android's app sandbox for its private local data and uses secure network connections for its configured online content. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Content hosts are independent services and are responsible for their own security practices.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when Kuper's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates when the current version was last updated. If a change materially affects how the app handles information, we will provide an appropriate notice in the app or through its store listing where practical.
